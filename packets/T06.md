## Acceptance evidence

A user can distinguish claim, effective units and as-is payouts; changing selected version updates ancestor denominator correctly. Every node is inspectable without graph dragging. Invalid/incomplete/offline states are honest. Screenshots and actual behavior are reviewed. No ownership-certified or permanent-devnet badge.

Tests must fail on the corresponding broken behavior, not merely assert that a screen renders. Record actual commands/results and commit generated vectors/reports where specified. Do not use mocks to certify the very integration this packet is meant to prove.

Use local fixtures/adapters and test networks to complete all unblocked work. No purchases, production signatures/funding, binding consent or real payouts without approval. A human account/credential gate is not permission to stop unrelated implementation. Preserve current work and the exact smallest missing action in status files.
